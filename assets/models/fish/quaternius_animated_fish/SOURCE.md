# Quaternius Animated Fish

Selected GLB models from the Animated Fish Bundle by Quaternius.

- Source: https://poly.pizza/bundle/Animated-Fish-Bundle-44zhHN1UbT
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- License URL: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Included: Tuna, Red Snapper, and Piranha

The original bundle contains more than 50 animated low-poly fish. These three
species are included to provide visual variation without importing unused
fishing props and models.
