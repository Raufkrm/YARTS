## Deer and Cow

Deer and Cow from **Ultimate Animated Animal Pack**, by **Quaternius**.
Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html
License: CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Adapted for Yarts: meter scale, welded topology, reduced LODs, a vertex-color
palette, renamed animation clips and loop settings. Original animation data
preserved. Cow eye attachment roots are capped and each closed component is
reduced separately to retain solid eyes at every LOD. Attribution is appreciated
but not required by CC0.
